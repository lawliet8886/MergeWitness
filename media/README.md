# MergeWitness media provenance

`mergewitness_demo.mp4` is the 96-second, 1920×1080 H.264/AAC judge video. It
shows the running browser lab throughout: comparison at 00:12, four-snapshot
matrix and tenant counterexample, the Bob evidence and repair tabs, and a fresh
candidate run at 01:15. The real IBM Bob IDE task 1 and 2 screenshots appear as
labeled picture-in-picture evidence. The final call to action names the live
demo. Full FFmpeg decode, duration, dimensions, and audio were checked after
rendering.

`capture_ui_actions.py` reproduces the 96 actual browser states from a running
local demo; its output is ignored under `.ui-action-frames/` because it is a
temporary 13 MB render input. `build_final_video.py` composites those states
with the real Bob screenshots and burns `demo_captions_en.srt`, whose exact
sentence timings come from `build_captions.py`. If the capture sequence is
absent, the builder uses the six checked-in `ui-final-*.png` application states.
The six captures are also an inspectable visual record of the final desktop UI.

`narration_sulafat.wav` is the English narration synthesized with Google Cloud
Gemini-TTS voice Sulafat. `narration.txt` is its exact input, and
`narration_sulafat.json` records text/audio hashes, duration, model, voice, and
estimated generation cost. The same audio was reused in the final edit.

`cover.png` is the exact-text 1200×630 LabLab poster generated by
`build_cover.py`. The four cards are independent snapshot results, not a
sequential branch graph. The material describes a synthetic demonstration and
does not claim that one passing probe certifies every merge as safe.
